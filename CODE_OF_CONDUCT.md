# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This is a small project and we want it to stay a pleasant place to work on.

- Be respectful. Disagree with the code or the idea, never with the person.
- Assume good faith, and say what you mean plainly.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests or anywhere the project is discussed.
- Keep private information private: do not post anyone's data, and redact your own before you share a transcript or output.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments and contributions that break these rules, and may ban people who keep breaking them. To report a problem, contact a maintainer through GitHub, privately if you prefer.
